Diving into the world of custom mechanical keyboards has been an exciting journey. Here's my experience building my first custom keyboard.

## The Components

- Case: Tofu60 Aluminum
- PCB: DZ60RGB-ANSI V2
- Switches: Gateron Black Inks (lubed with Krytox 205g0)
- Stabilizers: Durock V2 (lubed and holee modded)
- Keycaps: GMK WoB

## Build Process

The build process was both challenging and rewarding. Here are the steps I followed:

1. Testing the PCB
2. Lubing switches
3. Modding stabilizers
4. Assembly
5. Programming with QMK

## Sound Test and Typing Feel

The keyboard has a deep, thocky sound profile thanks to:
- Foam modding
- Lubed switches
- Properly tuned stabilizers

## Lessons Learned

1. Always test your PCB first
2. Take your time with switch lubing
3. Don't overtighten case screws
4. Proper stabilizer tuning is crucial
